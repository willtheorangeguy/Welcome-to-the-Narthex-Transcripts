• The idea of being exiles in a foreign land is present throughout the Old Testament and still relevant today as Christians living in the world.
• Jeremiah's letter to the exiles in Babylon emphasizes settling down, marrying, having children, praying for the city's prosperity, and trusting that if it prospers, they will prosper too.
• Forgetting God's commands and promises is a recurring theme from Adam to the present day, leading to sin.
• The importance of reading and remembering God's word is emphasized, as forgetting is seen as a precursor to sinning.
• Remembering is not just recalling facts but involves hearing, obeying, living, being, standing in God's grace, and responding when called by name.
• God remembers His people despite their forgetfulness, setting the stage for the next act of redemption through Jesus Christ.
